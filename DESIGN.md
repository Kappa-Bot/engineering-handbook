# Design entrypoint

The Engineering Handbook owns shared quality and accessibility guidance, **not a common portfolio art direction**. Use [Design Context Layering](patterns/design-context-layering.md) and the consumer repository's existing design contract first.

## Operator console interaction and material

For authenticated CRM and internal workspaces, use [Operator Console Shell](patterns/operator-console-shell-interactions.md) for navigation/theme/route mechanics and [Operator Glass](patterns/operator-glass-surface-material.md) for the owner-preferred *candidate* Glassmorphism treatment: restrained translucent navigation, **solid data surfaces** and clear elevated tasks. The material Pattern is optional until the consumer's active design contract explicitly adopts it; it is not an instruction to re-theme existing products or public websites.

## MelodIQ Systems corporate identity and product naming

For MelodIQ-branded corporate surfaces only, use [the canonical Design DNA](brands/melodiq-systems/DESIGN.md), [numerical tokens](brands/melodiq-systems/tokens.json) and [the frozen v2 asset inventory](brands/melodiq-systems/assets.v2.json).

Product names and their migration boundaries are governed by [MelodIQ product portfolio naming](brands/melodiq-systems/PRODUCT_PORTFOLIO_NAMING.md). This does not import one visual theme into all products.

The contract preserves the owner-approved M–Q mark and melodic Q tail, differentiates approved identity from implementation defaults and unfinished exports, and protects Gmail MAIN's footer-only compact styling. The original asset archive is stored in restricted Drive; do not publish its private email/contact material.

This is an entrypoint, not another copy of the brand contract. It does not recolor MelodIQ Church OS, other verticals, Platform Admin or tenant products. Those products retain their existing design authority and require explicit adoption before using this theme.
