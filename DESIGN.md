# Design entrypoint

The Engineering Handbook owns shared quality, accessibility and the integrated **Unslop design reconstruction method**, not a universal portfolio art direction. Start with the [UI Standard](standards/ui-ux-quality-baseline.md#1-product-specific-design-and-reconstruction), [Design Context Layering](patterns/design-context-layering.md), [Frontend Quality Review](playbooks/frontend-quality-review.md), the [visual-direction catalog](patterns/visual-direction-catalog.md) when material is in scope, and the consumer's current evidence/contract. Within an authorized redesign, an old design contract is a revisable input, not a frozen skin: replace weak decisions and record their supersession. Current task constraints and nonvisual facts remain binding.

## Operator console interaction and material

For authenticated CRM and internal workspaces, the [visual-direction catalog](patterns/visual-direction-catalog.md) makes **Glassmorphism the starting default for new or already-authorized material redesigns of internal consoles with no current explicit local choice**; [Operator Glass](patterns/operator-glass-surface-material.md), [Accessible Neumorphism](patterns/operator-neumorphic-surface-material.md) and solid/editorial surfaces are distinct selectable treatments. [Operator Console Shell](patterns/operator-console-shell-interactions.md) supplies **independent optional interaction mechanics**. A catalog default never overrides local `DESIGN.md`, forces a style onto an existing product, creates a live user toggle or gives permission to edit another product. Choose against the real journey and rendered evidence.

## MelodIQ Systems corporate identity and product naming

For MelodIQ-branded corporate surfaces only, use [the canonical Design DNA](brands/melodiq-systems/DESIGN.md), [numerical tokens](brands/melodiq-systems/tokens.json) and [the frozen v2 asset inventory](brands/melodiq-systems/assets.v2.json).

Product names and their migration boundaries are governed by [MelodIQ product portfolio naming](brands/melodiq-systems/PRODUCT_PORTFOLIO_NAMING.md). This does not import one visual theme into all products.

The contract preserves the owner-approved M–Q mark and melodic Q tail, differentiates approved identity from implementation defaults and unfinished exports, and protects Gmail MAIN's footer-only compact styling. The original asset archive is stored in restricted Drive; do not publish its private email/contact material.

This is an entrypoint, not another copy of the brand contract. It does not recolor MelodIQ Church OS, other verticals, Platform Admin or tenant products. Those products retain their existing design authority and require explicit adoption before using this theme.
