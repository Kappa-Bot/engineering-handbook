# MelodIQ Systems — OpenDesign profile

> Derivative for OpenDesign. **Not a new brand authority.**
> Canonical: `brands/melodiq-systems/DESIGN.md` v1.6 and `tokens.json` v1.0.0.

## Scope
Corporate MelodIQ Systems communications and presentation surfaces only.
Do not apply to ChurchOS, JobOps, Platform Admin or a customer's tenant identity.
A discreet corporate endorsement does not transfer the corporate palette to a product.

## Atmosphere
Precise, deliberate and calm. Technology through systems and readable structure; melody through restrained rhythm, not musical wallpaper. Avoid AI-sounding slogans, floating KPI wallpaper, generic neon gradients, glow or card-in-card compositions.

## Brand identity and assets
Write **MelodIQ Systems** in text, including alt text and metadata. The approved logo is the **restricted v8 two-root raster pack**; it is not embedded here. Never draw, trace, approximate, reconstruct or regenerate it. Use approved local asset bytes only after source-hash verification in the canonical brand contract. A typographic company name in this starter is ordinary text, not a reconstructed logo.

## Colors
Light: paper `#FAF8FF`, ink `#1F1B2E`, elevated white `#FFFFFF`, violet `#6D28D9`, muted `#625A70`, quiet border `#E5DEF2`. Dark uses ink canvas, deep `#2E1065` surface and lilac `#C4B5FD` accent. All numeric values and semantic aliases live in `tokens.css`, derived from the corporate token JSON. Use violet sparingly; never for every status.

## Typography
Manrope for headings; Inter for body and UI. If unavailable, system-ui. Body baseline 16px with 1.6 line-height; headings use controlled negative tracking. Do not distribute fonts or infer the logo wordmark font from the artwork.

## Layout and density
Maximum content 75rem. Gutters 20/32/48px at mobile/tablet/desktop. Sections 48px mobile and 80px desktop. Keep reading lines generally at most 68ch. Prefer aligned rows, editorial space and meaningful groups to decorative containers.

## Components and states
Minimum standard control height 44px and focus-visible affordances. Primary action: solid violet, 8px radius, explicit verb; panels: 12px radius. Loading, empty, error and success have distinct truthful behavior. No fake statistics, fabricated customer logos or decorative progress.

## Motion
Use motion only for causal feedback: around 120ms feedback, 180ms disclosure and up to 240ms entrance; maximum 8px travel. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No ambient looping logo effects.

## Accessibility
Check actual contrast, keyboard focus, zoom/reflow, screen-reader semantics and real mobile touch reach. Normal text must meet WCAG 4.5:1 where applicable; large text 3:1; meaningful non-text boundaries 3:1. Lilac on paper is decorative, not body text.

## Copy and commercial truth
Spanish (Spain), concise, useful and concrete. Describe proposed scope as **proposal**, not accomplished capability. Never invent clients, savings, integrations, certifications, NIF, legal entity suffix or availability. Drafts must be reviewed before delivery.

## Provenance and updates
This folder is an OpenDesign transport projection, not the source of truth. The source documents and restricted logo pack remain governed by the Handbook. Refresh this package when the source changes, verify numeric equality, and evaluate visual fidelity after import.
