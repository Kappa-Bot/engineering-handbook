## Design judgment and contradictory evidence

The [design-context review](../patterns/design-context-layering.md#anti-slop-review) turns symptoms into decisions. The evidenced mechanisms are template reuse without a job (CW-01/02/04), visual claims outrunning real state (CW-03/09/19) and effects delaying available information (CW-12/13). Unnecessary card nesting, repeated badges, generic claims and filler copy are additional diagnostic hypotheses. The audits did not establish that a model caused them or that every COGOP page showed them. Compare a contested treatment using the same content, first task and accessible states. Keep expressive type, imagery, surfaces and motion when the comparison supports the product's intent.

The [internal donor audit](internal-ui-pwa-donor-audit.md#product-ux-comparison--2026-10-26) shows other implementations of the underlying needs. MelodIQ-Web's cancellable entrances and IsmaelVizcaya-Web's retained timer state support independently controlled enhancement. MovOps' inspector focus return and DentalOS' guarded history support contextual recovery. The console curtain remains a visual precedent. Its product-specific control position and preference persistence provide a different kind of evidence from COGOP's hit testing. The CSS View Transitions specification explains rendering suppression during capture and hit-test exclusion of participating elements. The adopted pattern accounts for both stages. APG supports disclosure and combobox semantics. WCAG supplies the applicable focus, contrast, name and reflow criteria. The product evidence supplies the interrupted sequences to test.

The frontend Playbook now links timed marketing scenes to the existing hero control and WCAG contract. Its earlier blanket visible-pause wording conflicted with that contract. A compliant stopping mechanism remains an accessibility acceptance question where WCAG SC 2.2.2 applies. Product control approval and observed compliance each need their own evidence.

**Kept local.** Palette and 60/30/10 composition, exact typography and section counts, paths and copy, the 600 ms right-to-left reveal, named motion values and local resume storage. **Rejected for promotion.** A ban on cards, glass or expressive motion, a mandatory animation duration, a global cold-load budget, case quotas and visual score thresholds. The reusable layers are two Patterns and scoped additions to the existing design/evidence/performance guidance and review procedure. The Standard links to these without a new quality mandate.

## Measurement limits

- In B908, four cold loads per build yielded long-task total medians of 231 → 372 ms and a candidate maximum of 913 ms. An independent eight-pair interleaved series yielded 164.5 → 191.5 ms and a candidate maximum of 257 ms. All eight candidate runs in the second series met the project's 500 ms check. Both series show a higher candidate median. Variation and build/configuration differences limit causal attribution. The first series remains evidence.
- B's theme and progress results describe frame callbacks and layout in the lab. LCP varied by route and series. Compositing, actual dropped or presented frames and field INP require appropriate instruments and representative conditions. Similar blur scroll samples provide no universal cost guarantee.
- A reports 156 Chromium screenshot states, selected multi-engine journeys, 194 unit/integration tests and 56 E2E checks. Its initial hosted smoke applies to the cited Preview SHA. B reports 195 tests and 56 E2E checks on the final product candidate, selected Chromium/Firefox/WebKit checks and 50 independent final scenarios. Private flows used controlled fixture responses. Device emulation and native tab zoom are distinct checks. Physical-device touch, representative lower-powered hardware, assistive technology, field Web Vitals and the live private storage/OAuth cycle remain unverified by these audits.
