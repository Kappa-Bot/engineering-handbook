## Material adaptations and exclusions

- **Spain and privacy:** a published business email, legitimate interest asserted for research, licensed database or verified deliverability does not itself authorize unsolicited electronic marketing. Check the applicable channel, relationship/permission, LSSI-CE requirements and exceptions (`src-boe-lssi`) separately from GDPR lawful basis, transparency, minimization, rights and retention (`src-eu-gdpr`). Keep the evidence of permission/exception and suppression handling local. This is a compliance gate, not a determination that any particular outreach is lawful.
- **Consent and tracking:** apply applicable cookie/storage/access consent requirements and current AEPD guidance (`src-aepd-cookie-guide`). A server-side route or hashing does not erase personal-data obligations or grant consent. Do not send prohibited identifying information to Google Analytics (`src-google-analytics-pii`); use an approved event/data contract rather than copying vendor snippets.
- **Conversion truth:** distinguish link clicks, successful form acceptance, verified delivery, booked/held meetings, qualified leads and revenue. A WhatsApp or telephone click proves neither conversation nor lead receipt. Failed submissions, reloads, retries and demo fixtures must not masquerade as successful conversions. Record destination evidence and limitations; consent refusal is not a tracking defect to bypass.
- **Public/private boundary:** the public Handbook contains procedures and sanitized examples, not lead lists, CRM exports, personal contacts, private pricing, customer interviews, confidential competitor dossiers, credentials or account IDs. Minimize local raw evidence and define its authorized processing/retention; public availability does not grant unlimited reuse.
- **Demo and search truth:** preserve deliberate `noindex`, demo labels, authenticated boundaries and owner launch decisions. Robots directives are not access control. Do not publish invented reviews, aggregate ratings, cases, addresses or maturity claims, nor treat crawler eligibility as actual indexing, ranking or an AI citation.
- **Evidence strength:** dated vendor studies, platform tables, benchmarks and upstream numeric examples are hypotheses/reference context, not client forecasts or acceptance thresholds. No universal menu size, click depth, SLA, lead score, uplift or A/B sample table becomes normative. Low traffic may require qualitative evaluation instead of a claimed experiment winner.
- **No expanded authority:** no domain skill/plugin installation, partner service, paid API, new account, scheduler, recurring monitor, enrichment, outbound transmission, DNS change, CRM workflow or product change follows from this integration. Existing authorized tools may be evaluated within task scope; unavailable metrics stay unavailable.

Nonselected modules and the full upstream catalog remain unadopted. Linked dependencies, external services and separately credited material require their own license, applicability, permission and supply-chain review before reuse. Do not execute bundled installers, CLIs, recipes or snippets simply because this reference links their repository.

## Evaluation scenarios

These are acceptance scenarios for review/tests, not claims of observed campaign outcomes:

1. **Spanish local-business opportunity:** research a small approved set of businesses using dated public evidence; retain minimal qualification facts privately. A public address or valid email does not pass the outbound gate. Internal drafts pending channel permission are marked not approved for sending; no send or paid enrichment.
2. **Noindex client demo:** improve positioning and a genuine WhatsApp prefill while preserving fictional-review labels, excluded indexing and truthful capability boundaries. Reject invented proof, a “message sent” success state and counting the click as a qualified lead.
3. **Live form and low traffic:** define accepted submission versus failure; test retries/deduplication and receipt in the authorized destination without real customer data or ad spend. Record `PASS` / `FAIL` / `NOT RUN`. Prespecify an experiment only with sufficient justified traffic; otherwise return an inconclusive result or bounded qualitative findings.

Delivery tests belong to the actual Handbook change and consumer release record. These scenarios do not establish business improvement, legal clearance or external-service behavior on their own.
