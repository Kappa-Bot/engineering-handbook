# SEO, local SEO and GEO pricing in Spain — evidence and packaging

## Research method and interpretation

This is a **non-normative market reference**, not an approved MelodIQ price list. Figures below are **published claims by agencies or consultant-authored market articles**, not transaction-level invoices, audited market averages or evidence of ROI. SEO offerings are heterogeneous: a €250/month listing update is not comparable to a €1,500/month technical, content, editorial and outreach program. VAT inclusion, setup fees, content production, tools, link placements and contractual minimums often are **not specified**; mark them **unknown**, do not infer "IVA incluido". Recheck the live offer and date before using a figure in a customer proposal.

**Provenance:** initial research captured the four named public sources in November 2026. The IngenieroSEO page was independently reopened and its published figures checked on 2026-11-13; the other pages' figures are retained from the research evidence and **require fresh direct confirmation** if quoted externally. This distinction prevents a failed fetch or a blog's "market average" from becoming a verified commercial fact.

## 2. Cost structure and capacity model

For each client calculate **planned direct hours** by role:

| Workstream | Setup example (hours) | Monthly example (hours) | Comments |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Discovery, competitor/intent map, access | 3–6 | 0.5–1 | Setup higher if many markets or profiles |
| Technical crawl, indexability, schema, QA | 5–14 | 1–4 | Complex migrations and ecommerce are separate scopes |
| Content briefs, editorial work and verification | 3–10 | 2–8 | Actual writing/translation/photo production may be separate |
| Local profile, reviews/citations | 2–6 | 1–3 | Requires owner access and eligibility |
| AI-search observations | 1–3 | 0.5–2 | Optional sampling; no guaranteed citation |
| Reporting, coordination and review | 2–4 | 1–2 | Include independent QA and owner communication |
| Contingency | 10–20% | 10–20% | Explicit buffer, not "unlimited SEO" |

These are **internal estimation examples**, not industry averages. Adjust for page count, technical debt, languages, legal review, integration complexity and client response latency.

**Pricing formula (planning):**

- `Direct cost = Σ(role hours × loaded hourly cost) + variable tools + external production + authorized vendor costs`.
- `Gross contribution = price before tax − direct cost`.
- `Contribution margin = gross contribution / price before tax`.
- `Minimum price for target contribution margin m = direct cost / (1 − m)`, for `0 ≤ m < 1`.
- Add overhead, acquisition cost, account management and rework to judge **operating profit**. Contribution margin is not company net margin.

**Worked example (illustrative, not a quotation):** 12 hours × €30 loaded cost/hour = €360; tools/variable production €40; direct cost €400. At €700/month excl. VAT, contribution = €300, margin ≈42.9%. At a target 40% contribution margin, minimum price ≈€666.67. If those 12 hours do not cover client approvals, QA and reporting, the estimate is under-scoped.

**Red flags:** fixed price without a page/market/tech assessment; "unlimited keywords" without work capacity; unclear ownership of accounts; paid backlinks disguised as organic; minimum ranking promises; 12-month lock-in without defined outputs; no QA; reporting based on vanity metrics; "GEO visibility guaranteed".

## 3. Proposed MelodIQ service architecture — **hypothesis, not approved tariff**

The following is an **internal starting model** for proposal testing. Final prices and public publication require MelodIQ commercial approval and client-specific scoping. Prices are **illustrative, before VAT**, not market facts.

| Offer | Illustrative setup | Illustrative recurring fee | Intended scope and typical monthly capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Local Foundation** | €490–900 once | €390–690/month | Single eligible business and one principal service area; site/GBP baseline, top issue fixes within agreed hours, 1 prioritized content improvement, local accuracy, monthly report; approximately 6–10h/month |
| **SME Growth** | €900–1,800 once | €790–1,490/month | Several service/intent clusters, regular technical/content implementation, internal links, local/national evidence, conversion analysis and AI visibility sampling; approximately 12–22h/month |
| **Commerce / Multi-market** | Scoped discovery €1,500+ | €1,500–3,500+/month | Large catalogs, facets, feeds, multilingual, multiple real locations or heavy integrations; capacity and deliverables quoted per complexity, often 25h+ |
| **Audit / migration / remediation** | Fixed quote by inventory and risk | None unless separately contracted | Reproducible diagnostic, issue ledger, implementation or migration/QA; do not sell as a perpetual retainer by default |
| **Advisory / training** | Defined workshop/assessment | Hour or hour bank | Strategy, coaching, independent review; **not** hands-on implementation unless explicitly included |

**Positioning:** AEO/GEO is integrated into truthful discoverability and measurement; an extra "AI visibility" line item must have a separately defined deliverable and honest sampling methodology, not a promise to be cited. Technical fixes may be one-time; ongoing retainers must deliver genuinely recurring work, not charge for monitoring an unchanged site indefinitely.

### Minimum contents of every commercial proposal

- Client and site/market scope; eligibility and verified business facts.
- Baseline and primary qualified outcome; channels included (Google Search, Maps, Bing, answer engines) and their limits.
- Exact initial audit, implementation priorities, page/content capacity, review/QA, reports and meeting cadence.
- Hours/capacity and how overages are approved; external account ownership and access requirements.
- **Not included**: Ads spend, purchases, tools/licences, photo/video shoots, translation, legal advice, site redesign, domain/DNS change, link placements, CRM rebuild, third-party profile edits without access.
- Setup fee, recurring fee, taxes/VAT status, payment schedule, review period, cancellation and asset/credential handoff.
- Acceptance criteria based on **delivered work**, not SERP position or guaranteed organic traffic.
- Privacy/consent and permitted data access, responsible contacts and change-control mechanism.
- Evidence and reports with `PASS`, `NOT RUN` and `OWNER ACTION` statuses.

## 4. Pricing by sector, risk and delivery effort

**Local home services:** more GBP, service pages, genuine field photography and call/quote conversion; fewer articles may be better than generic blogging. **Medical/legal/finance:** higher factual/legal QA and approval costs; price accordingly. **B2B/industrial:** deep subject matter interviews, cases and long attribution windows; fewer high-value leads. **Ecommerce:** inventory, templates, faceted navigation and feed integration; volume/technical risk dominate. **Multilingual tourism:** localization, real location availability, seasonality and multilingual QA. **SaaS:** technical docs, product pages and demo attribution; don't bill for fictional features.

Do not charge by **keyword count** alone. Use actual complexity, deliverable volume, review burden, market reach, platform constraints and customer value. Do not infer that a high-value sector automatically permits deceptive pricing.

## 5. Contract, review and commercial QA

- Quote an initial **scope envelope** and distinguish `fixed project`, `retainer` and `hour bank`.
- Define client responsibilities: factual sign-off, access delegation, approvals and response windows; a blocked profile is not agency non-performance.
- Record third-party changes, algorithm updates and demand shifts as risks outside agency control.
- Review capacity, effective hourly rate, scope creep and client outcomes after the first 30/60/90 days and quarterly.
- Client retains domain, content rights as contracted, analytics/profiles and source access; define transition/handoff.
- Avoid fabricated "minimum +10% traffic" contractual guarantees. It is acceptable to commit to a number of audited URLs, implemented fixes, reviewed pages, reporting dates and support response times.
- Track actual margins privately. **Do not commit customer prices, contracts, invoices or client-specific economics to the public Handbook.**

## 6. Refresh checklist

Before making a public comparison or changing MelodIQ's tariffs:

- [ ] Open each source URL and record exact capture date and page update date.
- [ ] Distinguish advertised entry fee, broad market estimate, individual quote and independently observed contract.
- [ ] Record VAT, setup, deliverables, hours, content/link spend, tools, region and minimum term; mark unknowns explicitly.
- [ ] Recompute internal loaded costs, review/QA time, utilization and margin assumptions.
- [ ] Compare like-for-like client complexity; do not anchor premium engineering service to a €99 listing-only offer.
- [ ] Obtain owner approval for the final public price list and commercial terms.
- [ ] Keep third-party vendor marketing assertions as **supporting research**, never a normative engineering rule.
