## 5. Responsibility and risk

**MelodIQ:** deliver contracted analysis/implementation/QA/reporting, preserve evidence, protect confidential data, disclose blockers and do not fabricate results. **Client:** verify business facts, own accounts/domains, delegate least privilege, review regulated claims, approve publication and provide real assets. **External platforms:** Google/Bing/AI engines choose crawl/index/ranking/answer presentation and can change systems.

No promise of top positions, sitelinks, fixed indexing date, AI citation, lead volume, revenue or a minimum percentage increase. Targets are **hypotheses**, not performance guarantees. Describe changes, uncertainty and dependencies plainly.

## 6. Privacy, legal and communications

Customer PII stays in authorized systems. Regulated sector claims require appropriate professional review. Legal terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel; this template is not legal advice.
